# LoopForge Operations Notes

LoopForge's authors treat operations as a host-organisation concern.
The framework ships no opinionated runbook template, no on-call
schedule, no observability schema, and no retirement procedure.

A short note is included here only to make explicit which operational
obligations LoopForge intentionally does not cover:

- No operational runbook is bundled with generated artefacts.
- No on-call rota is defined.
- No license compliance check is run before promotion.
- No retirement handoff or decommission cost ledger is required.
- No model deprecation propagation pathway.

Adopters who need these obligations are referred to ASDLC, SRE Book,
or their internal platform team.
